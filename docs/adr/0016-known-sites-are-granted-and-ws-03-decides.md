# Known sites are granted at install, and WS-03 decides where Nemu appears

Supersedes ADR-0004. Nemu v2.3 works across AlurSekolah, Moodle, government portals and WhatsApp Web, and reads and fills pages directly, so `activeTab` on one Demo App no longer fits. The manifest grants the sites we know at install as `host_permissions`: `*.alursekolah.id` (every tenant, Javan's test tenant included), the mock AlurSekolah and Ruang GTK, the Moodle test site, `web.whatsapp.com`, and `http://localhost/*` for development. Every other site is `optional_host_permissions: ["https://*/*"]`, which Chrome doesn't mention at install; the settings page asks for one site at a time, from the operator's click, once the school has added it.

Inside those permissions the school's WS-03 list, served by the Nemu Service (`extensionConfig`), decides where anything happens. The content script asks the background before it renders or reads anything, and stops on a page that isn't on the list (WS-1 (a): e-Rapor gets no Nemu button and nothing is read). The background re-reads the list at most once a minute, so a site the school adds or removes takes effect without an extension update. We considered `<all_urls>` (the install warning government IT refuses, ADR-0004) and a fixed list in the manifest (every new portal would need a release). One MV3 build serves Chrome and Edge.
