# Photo jobs live in R2, and the laptop polls for them

A grade-book photo (from the WhatsApp webhook or an upload) is stored with its job state under `inbox/<teacher>/<job>/` in a separate R2 bucket whose lifecycle rule deletes anything older than a day; acknowledging a job deletes it at once. The laptop lists its jobs and asks for one to be read, so the 25–40 s vision call runs inside that request rather than after a webhook response. We planned a Durable Object per teacher with a WebSocket push, but the Alchemy version in this repo binds Durable Objects through Effect-based workers, and our Worker is a plain Hono app; wiring it untested two days before the demo risked the deploy. R2 keeps the Service free of in-memory state (ADR-0004 still holds per request), and photos are never logged. The cost is polling latency of a few seconds; a Durable Object push can replace `inboxJobs` polling later without changing the job format.
