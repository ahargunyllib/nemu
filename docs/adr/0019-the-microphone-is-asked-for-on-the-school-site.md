# The microphone is asked for on the school site, where Nemu can point at Izinkan

PRD v2.3 (#268) wants Nemu to point at Chrome's **Izinkan** the first time the teacher talks. Until now the extension recorded in an offscreen document, so the permission belonged to the extension, and only an extension page could ask: the welcome page did, at install, and a hold without it opened that page in a new tab with "Izinkan di halaman Nemu yang baru terbuka, lalu coba lagi."

Now the content script records on the school site itself. The first hold there calls `getUserMedia` from the page, so Chrome's prompt hangs from the address bar of the tab she is using. Nemu's cursor glides to just right of it, level with its top button, with a Gelembung Tunjuk beside it, and stays until Chrome has an answer. Chrome's prompt is browser UI the page can't see, so Nemu points at its measured place: on Chrome for macOS it spans 126–447 px across, with the top button 301 px below the window's top edge, less the bars above the page (`outerHeight - innerHeight`). If she allows it, that hold isn't recorded, and Nemu tells her to hold again. If the site is blocked, Nemu doesn't ask, since Chrome would refuse at once. It shows a Kartu Info with three steps instead: the icon left of the address bar, then Izinkan for Mikrofon, then Muat ulang (#280). These steps work because the permission is the site's, shown in that same address bar. The background now only sends the recording to `speechTranscribe`. The offscreen document, the `offscreen` permission and the welcome page's microphone step are gone.

We considered keeping the extension's permission and pointing on the welcome tab. That needs one Izinkan for every site, but the first hold sends her to another tab, and a blocked microphone would need steps about a page she isn't on. We chose one Izinkan per school site (AlurSekolah and Moodle each ask once), in the tab where she wants to talk. A site that sends `Permissions-Policy: microphone=()` would refuse the content script; none of the school sites Nemu runs on does. The measured place drifts with Chrome versions, page zoom and full screen, so the cursor may stand a little off the button; the Gelembung Tunjuk names the button, so the words still lead her there.
